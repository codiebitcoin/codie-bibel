# Built-in assets

- `jesus.vrm` — the app's own figure of Jesus, a VRM model titled
  "Jesus" from VRoid Hub (hub.vroid.com, model 307680147834872455),
  recovered through CODE27's workshop resource service. It wears the
  Jesus persona until the reader uploads a model of their own. Its use
  follows the terms the model carries on VRoid Hub.
- `jesus-voice.wav` — a synthesized blessing (KJV words, Apple's
  Daniel en-GB system voice, 18 s). It is the seed the device clones
  the Jesus persona's voice from, once, on the reader's own account.
- `default-figure.vrm` — pixiv's three-vrm sample humanoid model, from
  github.com/pixiv/three-vrm (MIT). It is the figure the monk wears,
  and every persona wears until a model of their own is given.
