# Built-in assets

- `jesus.vrm` — the app's own figure of Jesus, a VRM model titled
  "Jesus" from VRoid Hub (hub.vroid.com, model 307680147834872455),
  recovered through CODE27's workshop resource service. Its terms on
  VRoid Hub allow redistribution, alteration and corporate use. It
  wears the Jesus persona until the reader uploads a model of their
  own.
- `monk.vrm` — the monk persona's figure, a vested cleric from VRoid
  Hub (model 8631357567214777894, "アバター_神父服モデル" by ALL・nee・t),
  recovered through the same service. **The creator's terms on VRoid
  Hub restrict use of this model; it is bundled at CODE27's own
  decision** — replace it with a model of your own where its terms
  would not suit your use.
- `jesus-preview.png`, `monk-preview.png` — rendered from those two
  very models (three.js + @pixiv/three-vrm).
- `jesus-voice.wav` — a synthesized blessing (KJV words, Apple's
  Daniel en-GB system voice, 18 s). It is the seed the device clones
  the Jesus persona's voice from, once, on the reader's own account.
- `default-figure.vrm` — pixiv's three-vrm sample humanoid model, from
  github.com/pixiv/three-vrm (MIT). It is the figure a persona falls
  back to should its own model be missing.
